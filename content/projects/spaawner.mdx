## SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Spaawner is a launchpad launcher. An owner creates and publishes a branded launchpad, customizing its design, access rules, supply, launch economics, and creator tax policy. Token creators then use that launchpad's public surface to launch tokens under its settings.

Each launchpad gets its own public storefront and on-chain policy and revenue accounting, while all launchpads share one token launch engine.

## LAUNCHPAD-TO-TOKEN FLOW

The owner publishes a launchpad through the shared factory, creating a dedicated Tenant Launchpad contract. That contract enforces the owner's launch access and economic settings. Token creators select an approved pair and launch a fixed-supply token through the launchpad, which records attribution and fee accounting on-chain.

Each token starts on a Pons V2-style curve and can graduate through a retryable flow to a full-range Uniswap v4 position whose liquidity principal stays locked.

## ARCHITECTURE

**Launchpad builder and public launch surfaces**
The React and Vite application lets owners customize and publish launchpads, and gives token creators a branded surface for launching through them. A Hono API supports the application.

**Wallet and contracts**  
Dynamic authenticates launchpad owners; wagmi and viem handle wallet interactions. Solidity contracts define tenant policy, launches, and fee accounting, with Foundry for development.

**Event projection**  
Envio indexes contract events. A scheduled Cloudflare Worker projects them into Supabase PostgreSQL for discovery and public launch views.

**Data ownership**  
The chain is authoritative for ownership, policy, attribution, and economics. Supabase stores branding, drafts, discovery data, and rebuildable projections.

## ENGINEERING FOCUS

- Letting each owner launch a branded launchpad with its own rules and economics
- Attributing token launches and fee accounting to the launchpad used
- Restricting markets to curated native, stock-token, and token pairs
- Keeping curve fees denominated in the selected pair asset
- Recovering cleanly from interrupted liquidity graduation
